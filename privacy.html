Privacy Policy

BEYOND FRAME · Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the BEYOND FRAME application (“the app”, “we”) accesses, uses, stores, shares and deletes data, including data received from Google APIs. The app is a desktop application used by the BEYOND FRAME team to publish its own 3D animated videos to its own YouTube channel, Instagram account, TikTok account and VK community after a person reviews and confirms each upload.

1. Who the app is for

The app is used only by the BEYOND FRAME team with accounts the team owns. It has no public sign-up, no user accounts and no server: it runs on the team's own computer. It does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other people.

2. Data the app accesses

Google user data (YouTube)

Permission (scope)Data accessedWhy
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload Permission to upload videos to the connected channel, and the id of each uploaded video To upload the video file with the title, description, tags and privacy setting the person confirmed
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly The connected channel's id and title and its subscriber, view and video counts; the upload/processing status, privacy status and statistics (view, like and comment counts) of videos the app uploaded To show which channel is connected, whether each upload finished processing, the link to the video and how it performs
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl Permission to delete a video; used only for videos the app uploaded To delete a video the app uploaded when the person clicks “Delete” for that video and confirms it. The app never deletes or edits anything else
OAuth tokens Access token and refresh token issued by Google To make the API calls above without asking the owner to sign in every time

The app does not access the Google account's e-mail, contacts, profile details beyond the channel title, other videos, the content of comments, playlists, the list of subscribers, watch history or YouTube Analytics reports.

Meta (Instagram) data

The list of Facebook Pages managed by the signed-in owner, the id and username of the Instagram professional account linked to the chosen Page and its follower, following and media counts, a Page access token, the ids/links of media the app published, and the like, comment, view, reach, save and share counts of those media — used only to publish the confirmed Reel, show its link and statistics, and delete a Reel the app published when the person clicks “Delete” and confirms it.

TikTok data

The account's open id and display name and OAuth tokens — used only to send the confirmed video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft and show the upload status. With user.info.stats the app reads the account's follower, following, like and video counts, only to show them to the team. With video.list the app reads the account's recent public videos (id, caption, link) and the view, like, comment and share counts of the videos it uploaded, only to show their statistics.

VK data

Through VK ID, with the accesses video, wall and groups: the VK ID tokens and device id, the signed-in user's id, the id, name, address and member count of the one community the team configured (and whether the user administers it), and the ids, links and view, like, comment and repost counts of the videos the app uploaded — used only to upload the confirmed video to that community (with a wall post when chosen), show its link and statistics, and delete a video the app uploaded when the person clicks “Delete” and confirms it.

Files

The video files and texts (title, description, hashtags) that the team prepared for publishing.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the app's feature: uploading the team's reviewed videos to the team's own accounts, reporting the result and statistics of those videos, and deleting them on request. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, analytics, credit or lending decisions, and it is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Nobody reads the data except the BEYOND FRAME team member operating the app, and only to publish and check the team's own videos.

4. Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens and app credentials are stored only on the team's computer, encrypted in the macOS Keychain.
  • Upload records (video ids, links, statuses) are stored in local files on the same computer.
  • No data is stored on any BEYOND FRAME server or third-party storage. Logs are written locally with tokens removed.
  • All communication with Google, Meta and TikTok uses HTTPS.
  • TikTok sign-in returns through the page https://dclxxxvi.github.io/oauth/tiktok/, which only forwards TikTok's one-time sign-in code to the app on the same computer; it stores and sends nothing elsewhere.

5. Sharing and transfer

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer user data to any third party. Data received from a platform is sent back only to that same platform as part of the upload (for example, the video and its title are sent to YouTube). Data received from Google APIs is never transferred to Meta, TikTok or anyone else.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

BEYOND FRAME's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens are kept until the account is disconnected in the app (“Disconnect”), which deletes them from the Keychain immediately.
  • Upload records are kept as the team's publishing history and are deleted together with the video's folder.
  • Access can be revoked at any time: Google — myaccount.google.com/permissions; Meta — Facebook Settings → Business integrations; TikTok — Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access the account.
  • To request deletion of any data held by the app, contact us (below); it will be deleted within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.
