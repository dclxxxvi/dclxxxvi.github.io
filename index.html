BEYOND FRAME

BEYOND FRAME is a desktop application that publishes the BEYOND FRAME channel's own short 3D animated films to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

What the app does

The BEYOND FRAME team renders short cinematic films in Blender. When a film is finished, the app shows a preview of the video together with its title, description and hashtags. A person reviews them, chooses the platforms and clicks Publish. The app then uploads that video file to the team's own channel and accounts using each platform's official API and shows whether each upload succeeded, with a link to the published video.

How the app uses Google data

The app connects to one YouTube channel — the BEYOND FRAME channel — with the owner's permission (Google OAuth). It uses the YouTube Data API only to:

  • upload videos to that channel (youtube.upload), with the title, description, tags and privacy setting the person confirmed; and
  • read the channel's name and the processing status of the videos it uploaded (youtube.readonly), to show the result and the link to the video.

The app does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments, playlists, subscribers or personal data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Content

All videos published with the app are original 3D animations made by the BEYOND FRAME team (stylized environments, objects and abstract scenes). The app does not generate images or video with AI. It is not used to create, upload or distribute sexual, intimate or non-consensual content of any kind, including AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery, and it does not depict real people. Published content follows the YouTube Community Guidelines.

Who can use it

The app is used only by the BEYOND FRAME team, on its own computer, with its own accounts. There is no sign-up and no public user account. It runs locally; there is no BEYOND FRAME server that stores user data.
